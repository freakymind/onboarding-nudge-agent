ONBOARDING MESSAGING & NUDGE SYSTEM — PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================


1. THE PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Customer and business onboarding in financial services is a multi-step,
document-heavy process that routinely stalls. Applications sit idle for days
or weeks because:

  a) Customers do not know what is expected of them next.
     They submit an application and then hear nothing. Required documents,
     identity checks, or signatures are requested once via email and forgotten.
     Without timely, repeated, channel-appropriate reminders the customer
     disengages and the application decays.

  b) Internal operations staff lose visibility of stuck applications.
     Case officers manage large queues. When an application requires a manual
     review, an approval, or an outbound call, it can be buried under newer
     work. There is no systematic way to alert the right person at the right
     time that an application needs attention.

  c) Communication is fragmented across channels.
     Some customers respond to email, others to SMS, others to WhatsApp or
     Teams messages. Today there is no unified orchestration layer that
     decides which channel to use, falls back to an alternative when there
     is no response, and keeps a single audit trail of every interaction.

  d) There is no closed-loop feedback.
     Messages are sent but the system does not track whether the customer
     acted on them. There is no mechanism to automatically check application
     progress after a nudge is sent and to escalate if the application
     remains stalled.

  e) Escalation is manual and inconsistent.
     When a customer does not respond to an initial outreach, it is left to
     individual staff to remember to follow up. There are no configurable
     rules that say "if no response via email within 48 hours, send an SMS;
     if still no response after 72 hours, escalate to a phone call."

The cumulative effect is longer onboarding times, higher drop-off rates,
increased operational cost, regulatory risk from incomplete KYC/AML checks,
and a poor customer experience that damages brand trust.


2. WHAT THIS SYSTEM SOLVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Onboarding Messaging & Nudge System is a centralised orchestration
platform that automates, tracks, and optimises every communication sent
during the onboarding lifecycle — to customers and to internal staff.

It addresses each failure mode above:

  Problem (a) — Customer silence
  > The system maps every onboarding event (application submitted, document
  > pending, review complete, etc.) to one or more outbound messages. Each
  > message is sent via the most appropriate channel, using a pre-approved
  > template with dynamic personalisation. The customer always knows what
  > to do next.

  Problem (b) — Internal staff blind spots
  > Role-based notification routing ensures that the right operations staff
  > are alerted when an application reaches a stage that requires their
  > input. Notifications are targeted by role (Ops Manager, Reviewer,
  > Compliance Officer) so that no application falls through the cracks.

  Problem (c) — Channel fragmentation
  > A unified routing engine supports SMS, Email, Microsoft Teams, and
  > WhatsApp from a single configuration interface. Routing rules define
  > which events trigger which channels, with conditions based on
  > application type, customer segment, time of day, and prior response
  > history.

  Problem (d) — No closed-loop tracking
  > Every message is logged with full delivery and response metadata.
  > The system continuously ingests application status updates from the
  > upstream onboarding platform and correlates them with sent messages
  > to determine whether a nudge was effective.

  Problem (e) — Manual, inconsistent escalation
  > Configurable escalation chains define automatic fallback behaviour.
  > If a customer does not respond to an email within N days, the system
  > automatically sends an SMS. If there is still no response, it
  > escalates to the next channel in the chain — without any manual
  > intervention.

  Ad-hoc messaging
  > Operations staff can also send manual messages at any time by
  > selecting an application, choosing a channel, and picking a template.
  > These ad-hoc messages are logged alongside automated ones for a
  > complete audit trail.


3. KEY BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Faster onboarding completion
    Automated, timely nudges reduce the average time from application
    submission to completion. Customers are prompted to act before they
    disengage.

  Lower drop-off rates
    Multi-channel escalation ensures that customers are reached on the
    channel they are most likely to respond to, reducing abandonment.

  Reduced operational overhead
    Staff no longer need to manually track which customers need follow-up
    or remember to switch channels. The system handles sequencing, timing,
    and escalation automatically.

  Complete audit trail
    Every message — automated or manual — is logged with timestamp,
    channel, recipient, template used, delivery status, and response
    status. This supports regulatory compliance and internal reporting.

  Improved staff productivity
    Role-based internal notifications surface the right work to the right
    person. Staff spend less time searching for stalled applications and
    more time progressing them.

  Data-driven optimisation
    Analytics dashboards expose channel performance, response rates by
    event type, escalation frequency, and time-to-action metrics. Teams
    can continuously refine their messaging strategy based on real data.

  Configurable without code changes
    Events, channels, routing rules, templates, staff roles, and
    escalation chains are all managed through an administrative interface.
    Business users can adapt the system to new products, regulatory
    requirements, or process changes without developer involvement.

  Consistent customer experience
    Templates ensure that every customer receives clear, on-brand,
    compliant communication regardless of which staff member or automated
    process triggers the message.


4. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Onboarding fails when communication is late, inconsistent, or invisible.
This system makes every onboarding communication timely, multi-channel,
trackable, and automatically escalating — so that customers complete
faster, staff work smarter, and the organisation retains full visibility
and control over the process.
